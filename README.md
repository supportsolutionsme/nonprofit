# Support Solutions Website

This repository contains a nonprofit website built to replicate the look and feel of the Support Solutions template. The site provides a digital presence for Support Solutions and showcases its mission to inspire a culture of possibilities for individuals with intellectual disabilities.

## About Support Solutions
Support Solutions is a nonprofit organisation founded in 1997 and serving the state of Maine. Its mission is to create a culture of possibilities for individuals with intellectual disabilities and to empower adults to live meaningful lives. The organisation is built on values of economic and social justice, outstanding management, environmental responsibility and person‑centred care.

## Features
- **Hero Section** – introduces the organisation’s mission and encourages visitors to explore services or join the team.
- **About Section** – outlines who Support Solutions is and lists core values.
- **Impact Section** – highlights key facts such as the founding year (1997) and state‑wide service.
- **Services Section** – describes the three primary programmes:
  - **Residential Housing** – provides 24/7 services in homes where individuals are encouraged to take ownership and style their environments. Training includes self‑care, communication, independent living skills, behaviour management and community living skills.
  - **Shared Living** – a programme where adults with autism, intellectual disabilities or acquired brain injury live with host families. This arrangement is grounded in connection, community and possibility.
  - **Creative Trails Programme** – experiential programming that explores art, agriculture, culinary, equestrian and outdoor experiences.
- **Stories of Impact** – includes inspirational quotes and testimonials.
- **Careers Section** – invites visitors to join the organisation, listing benefits such as comprehensive training, competitive salaries, flexible schedules and a supportive team.
- **Contact Section** – provides the organisation’s address (29 Lowell St., 5th Floor, Lewiston, ME 04240), phone number ((207) 795‑0672) and a link to the official website.

## Usage
1. Open `index.html` in a web browser to preview the site.
2. To customise the content, edit the `index.html` and `styles.css` files.
3. For deployment, you can host the site on GitHub Pages or another static host.
