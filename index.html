Residential & Community Services

Rooted in Compassion. Built on Purpose.

For over twenty‑five years, Support Solutions has provided personalised support to adults with intellectual disabilities, autism and brain injury. Our mission is to foster dignity and independence through a blend of residential, creative, community and shared living services across Maine.
